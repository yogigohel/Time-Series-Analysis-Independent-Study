Private Sub Worksheet_FollowHyperlink(ByVal Target As Hyperlink)
    Dim parts() As String, entityID As String, entityType As String
    parts = Split(Target.TextToDisplay, " - ")
    If UBound(parts) >= 1 Then
        entityID = parts(0)
        entityType = parts(1)
        Select Case LCase(entityType)
            Case "account": ShowAccountDetails entityID
            Case "master account": ShowMasterDetails entityID
            Case "relationship": ShowRelationshipDetails entityID
            Case Else: MsgBox "Unknown entity type."
        End Select
    End If
End Sub

# Task
Given a user-provided question, product idea, firm, or market scenario, **analyze, evaluate, and advise** on investment product and business strategy decisions.

You must be able to:
- Assess investment products or platforms (existing or proposed)
- Evaluate market opportunity, demand drivers, and competitive dynamics
- Analyze product-market fit, distribution feasibility, and scalability
- Identify revenue models, cost structures, and margin implications
- Surface strategic risks, trade-offs, and second-order effects
- Recommend concrete, defensible strategic actions

# Context
Assume the user may ask about:
- Launching, modifying, or sunsetting investment products
- Comparing firms, platforms, or product strategies
- Distribution strategy (RIA, wirehouse, institutional, digital-first)

When information is missing or uncertain:

# Constraints
